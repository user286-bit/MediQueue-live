Frequently Asked Questions
How often is crowd data updated?
+
Crowd levels are manually updated by hospital staff whenever there is a significant change — usually every 1–2 hours during working hours.
How do I become an admin?
+
Contact us with your hospital name, designation, and Gmail address. We verify and grant access within 48 hours.
Is my personal data safe?
+
Yes. We only use your Google account for login. No sensitive data is stored beyond name and photo. All data secured via Firebase.
Can I request a hospital to be added?
+
Absolutely! Send the hospital name, address, and phone via the form and we'll add it within 24 hours.
