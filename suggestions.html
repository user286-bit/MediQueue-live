💡 Smart Visit Tips
🕗
Best Hours to Visit
Hospitals are least crowded between 8–9 AM and after 4 PM. Avoid 10 AM–12 PM peak hours.
📅
Best Days of the Week
Tuesdays and Thursdays are typically less busy than Mondays and Fridays.
📋
Carry Documents Ready
Keep Aadhar, insurance card, and previous prescriptions ready to save registration time.
📞
Call Ahead
Many hospitals offer phone bookings. A quick call can cut waiting time significantly.
